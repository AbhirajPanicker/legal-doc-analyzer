Intelligent Legal Document Understanding & Analysis

LexAnalyzer is an enterprise-grade AI system designed for legal counsel, compliance analysts, and contract professionals to rapidly ingest, analyze, extract risks, detect blind spots, and query complex legal agreements with grounded source transparency.

1. What Does LexAnalyzer Do?

Reviewing lengthy commercial agreements, leases, NDAs, and corporate filings often takes hours of manual effort. LexAnalyzer automates contract intelligence through high-precision Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), allowing users to extract key terms, evaluate risks, pinpoint drafting omissions, and ask natural language questions.

Layer Technology Purpose & Responsibility
Frontend React 18 · Vite · Vanilla CSS High-performance responsive dashboard with dark-mode navigation, interactive clause audit, and clean reporting cards.

3. Key Features Breakdown

🔍 Contract Blind-Spot Detector

Advanced Audit Engine

A proactive legal auditing tool that scans documents to identify hidden contractual liabilities:

  • Potential Gaps: Identifies missing liability caps, notice and cure periods, and absent default remedies.
  • Ambiguity: Detects vague standards ("best efforts", "reasonable efforts") and indefinite timelines ("promptly", "from time to time").
  • Missing References: Flags citations to exhibits, schedules, and appendices that were referenced but omitted.
  • Needs Human Review: Flags unfulfilled placeholders ([TBD]) and unaddressed operational contingencies.

⚖️ Automated Clause & Risk Analysis

Risk Mitigation

Classifies 10 standard commercial contract clauses (Confidentiality, Indemnification, Limitation of Liability, Termination, Governing Law, Non-Compete, Force Majeure, IP, Payment, and Warranties). Assigns overall risk levels (High, Medium, Low) and alerts users to missing protective covenants.

📋 One-Click Executive Summarisation

Document Briefing

Generates a concise structured briefing capturing contracting parties, critical milestone dates, core obligations, payment/financial terms, governing jurisdiction, and a one-sentence executive takeaway.

💬 Document Chat with Verified Page Citations

Interactive Q&A

Allows attorneys and clients to ask plain-language questions against their contracts. Every answer is strictly grounded in the document text, referencing exact page numbers and excerpts to guarantee auditability.

🔒 Multi-Tenant Authentication & Strict Data Privacy

Security First

Full JWT authentication with bcrypt password security. Implements strict per-user document isolation: User A cannot see, search, or query documents uploaded by User B. Chat histories are private and scoped strictly to the authenticated account.
